About Dr. Cory J. Ortiz

Leadership, strategy, and innovation in workforce development and career & technical education.

I am a CTE administrator, educator, and strategist dedicated to expanding student access, building industry-driven pathways, and modernizing technical education across Alaska and beyond.

Professional Background

I currently serve as Division Director of the Alaska Vocational Technical Center (AVTEC), where I lead statewide technical training initiatives, institutional strategy, and workforce partnerships for the Alaska Department of Labor.

Previously, I was Academic Dean for the School of Career Education at the University of Alaska Southeast. In this role, I oversaw academic operations, secured major federal and industry grants, expanded CTE offerings, and drove significant enrollment growth.

My career spans K–12 work-based learning, university teaching, district-level CTE leadership, and service on state and national workforce education boards.

Areas of Expertise

CTE Leadership

Program development, enrollment strategy, and institutional growth through data-driven decision making.

Industry Partnerships

Building sustainable collaborations among employers, schools, training centers, and state agencies.

Innovation in Workforce Training

STEM/CTE research, drone-based learning, AI integration, and emerging technologies in education.

Research, Writing & Speaking

I am an active researcher and presenter focused on:

  • CTE teacher retention and workforce pipeline challenges
  • Drone-based STEM learning models
  • AI impacts and applications in technical education
  • Designing industry-aligned workforce pathways
  • Statewide strategies for rural workforce development

I regularly speak at conferences, lead workshops, and collaborate with organizations seeking to strengthen workforce systems.

Beyond Work

Outside of my professional roles, I serve in the U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary, support STEM outreach initiatives, and contribute to philanthropic and community development efforts statewide.
