Community Impact

Service, leadership, and event-driven community work focused on youth engagement and fundraising for childhood cancer research.

Key Highlights

  • $7,442 raised — Highland Junior High (May 13, 2016)
  • $6,000+ raised — Mt. Logan Middle School (Apr 18, 2015)
  • 30+ participants (students & staff)
  • Multi-year leadership organizing St. Baldrick’s events

Overview

    For multiple years I organized and led school-based St. Baldrick’s fundraising events that combined logistics, volunteer coordination, parent & administration outreach, PR, and on-the-ground leadership. Below are curated media features that document specific events, outcomes, and community response.
