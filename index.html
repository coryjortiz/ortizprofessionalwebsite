About Dr. Ortiz

Dr. Cory J. Ortiz is a highly experienced CTE administrator, educator, and workforce development strategist with a national record of improving program quality, expanding student access, and building industry-aligned training pathways.

He currently serves as Division Director of the Alaska Vocational Technical Center (AVTEC) for the Alaska Department of Labor, where he oversees academic operations, institutional strategy, partnerships, and statewide workforce training initiatives.

Previously, Dr. Ortiz was Academic Dean for the School of Career Education at the University of Alaska Southeast, where he led major enrollment growth, secured critical industry and federal funding, expanded program offerings, and forged high-impact partnerships with Alaska’s mining, construction, and maritime sectors.

His career includes roles in higher education, public school systems, and national professional organizations. He has taught at the university level, coordinated district-wide work-based learning initiatives, and contributed to state and national boards shaping CTE policy.

Dr. Ortiz is an active researcher, author, and presenter with work focused on teacher retention, drone-based STEM learning, AI in CTE, and industry-aligned program development. He is also a member of the U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary and a long-standing volunteer supporting STEM outreach and philanthropic initiatives.
